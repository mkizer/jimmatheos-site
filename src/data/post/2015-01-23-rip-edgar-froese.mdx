RIP Edgar. Everyone talks about Progressive music, but you were the truest innovator of this. Phaedra and Rubycon still stand, after 40+ years, on the vanguard of breaching the furthest boundaries of progressive music. Although later albums never realized the dreams of taking this to the next logical level and beyond, these two albums alone stand as proud accomplishments that will continue to influence artists throughout the decades.
