## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data

While using Our Service, We do not require user accounts or registration. However, if You choose to submit a message through our [Contact page](/contact), We ask You to provide certain personally identifiable information so we can respond to Your inquiry:

- Name
- Email address
- Message content

#### Usage Data

Standard Usage Data may be logged automatically by our hosting and content delivery infrastructure when accessing the Service.

Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, and other diagnostic data.

#### Local Storage, Cookies, and Third-Party Embeds

This Website does not set first-party advertising, analytics, or authentication cookies, and does not require a cookie consent banner. We use browser technologies and third-party embeds as follows:

- **Theme Preference (`localStorage`).** The Website stores your light or dark display preference locally in your browser's `localStorage` so your theme choice persists between visits. This data remains on your Device and is not transmitted to Our servers.
- **Contact Form Processing (Web3Forms).** Submissions sent through the [Contact page](/contact) are transmitted through Web3Forms (`https://web3forms.com`) to deliver your message to Us. Please review Web3Forms' privacy policy for details on how they handle form submissions.
- **Embedded Media (YouTube).** Video previews on the Website use a click-to-play facade so no connection to YouTube is made until you actively click to play a video. Once you click to play an embedded YouTube video, YouTube (Google) may set cookies or collect Usage Data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
