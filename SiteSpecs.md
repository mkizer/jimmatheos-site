## Gear

The current site has a gear section which consists of two photo albums (**Current Guitars** and **Past Guitars**). These each contain a photograph of each of his guitars which is clickable to expand to full size. Each photo has a short caption that displays when you hover over a photo. When you expand the photo, the same caption appears at the bottom of the photo. The photos are in: \src\assets\images\guitars
This could probably use a similar process as the Images page. Captions, current or past status could be specified in the associated metadata file, which would be used to put a guitar in the proper photo album (Current Guitars or Past Guitars). These two photo albums could also be combined into a single page, just separated into two sections.

In addition to the two photo albums, there are also a couple of regular HTML pages (**Amps / Effects** and **Live Rig Diagrams**). Both of these pages simply contain images and simple text lists that describe this area of equipment.
An HTML download of the original **Amps / Effects** page is here: "\import\Amps \_ Effects - Jim Matheos.html"
The original **Live Rig Diagrams** page merely displayed two images, now located here: "\src\assets\images\jim_guitargeek_2012.jpg" and "\src\assets\images\jim_guitarworld_2012.jpg"
